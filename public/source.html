Cinggu source

Cinggu Library on GitHub

Cinggu builds on the editing foundation of Bible Strong Avatar Lab. It adds our character family, 13 reference eye expressions, local drawing imports, custom head outlines and solid depth while retaining the familiar editor workflow. These tools help you develop expressions that belong to your own character.

This library adapts Bible Strong Avatar Lab by Stéphane Montlouis-Calixte. It is modified software, provided without warranty, under AGPL-3.0.
